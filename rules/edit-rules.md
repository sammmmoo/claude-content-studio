# Edit rules (every preset follows these)

These come from editing our own videos and measuring outliers. Change any of them in `my/taste.md` if your taste is different: yours win.

## The first second
- The **text hook** is on screen from frame 0, big, in the top third (12-33% down the frame). Never under the chin, never a strip at the very top where the app covers it.
- The person starts talking at about 0.1 s. Cut "okay, so" and any breath before the first word.
- Frame 0 shows what the video is about. A slight punch-in that zooms out with a **whoosh** on the first word works well.
- A short **riser** under the hook, ending on the first big word.

## Cutting
- Cut every gap over **0.1 s** between words. Keep the breath only where a pause lands a point.
- Cut false starts, repeats and restarts. Keep the best read of each line.
- **Punch-ins** (about 1.05x) only to hide an ugly jump cut or mark a new beat. Around 4 per 30 seconds at most, never two within 3 seconds.
- End on the last word. No dead air, no outro.

## Captions
- **2-3 words** at a time, natural phrases, never a word split from its partner.
- Sentence case or one clean style, one accent colour on the key word. No karaoke word-by-word colour flicker.
- Inside the safe zone: clear of the app's buttons on the right and the caption bar at the bottom.

## Graphics
- **Graphics show the thing being said:** a real screenshot, the real page, the real number. Real beats icons and emojis every time.
- One thing moving at a time. At least half the screen calm.
- Pop-ups appear and then sit still. No wobble.
- Keep the person's whole head in frame. Graphics sit around them, not over their face.

## Sound
- A **whoosh on every zoom** and big transition, a soft click or pop when a card appears. Quiet: under the voice, never over it.
- At most about 4 sound effects per 30 seconds, none on the call to action.
- No music on the master for Instagram if you add a trending sound in the app; keep a no-music version for that.
- Loudness around **-14 LUFS**, voice clear and level all the way through.

## Format
- 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264, AAC.
- Use only footage, screenshots and sounds you have the right to use. Never another creator's clip unless it's quoted, short and credited.
- Before you call it done, watch it start to finish, check every frame for anything private (a phone number, an email, a customer's name), and check the sound.
