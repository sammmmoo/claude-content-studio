# Edit presets

Each one is measured from a real creator's recent videos and their outliers (3x+ their median), from frame-by-frame contact sheets. Pick one per video in `my/style.md`. The creators named here inspired the styles; they aren't affiliated with this and haven't endorsed it. Every preset keeps the rules in `rules/edit-rules.md`. Want a style that isn't here? Add the creator to `my/creators.md`, run research-creators, and Claude writes a new preset in this file.

## 1. Wall
- Graphics live on a wall the camera glides along; you sit in a window at the bottom.
- One object per scene that builds in place. Black, white and grey plus one accent colour. 1-3 big words at a time, at least half the wall empty, one thing moving at once.
- Best for: lists and frameworks.

## 2. Top Card
- The graphic owns the top two-thirds; your head sits in the bottom third. One locked camera.
- Frame 0: a title card builds word by word, the key phrase boxed in the accent colour.
- A new graphic every 2-3 seconds, mostly real browser screenshots with the key sentence highlighted. Captions 1-2 words just above your head.
- Punch in to full face for the call to action.
- What wins for this style is the topic in the first 3 seconds, not the edit.
- Best for: tool and how-to videos.

## 3. Head Band
- You fill the lower 60-65% of the frame; a graphic band runs across the top 30-35%.
- Captions: 2-3 white capital words, the key word green, negatives red.
- A new screenshot, card or logo every 1.5-3 seconds. List items can go full screen on a white card ("#3 ...").
- Busy: 5-8 cuts per 10 seconds at the top end. Start slower.
- Best for: lists and news.

## 4. Red Box
- Frame 0: a red rounded box with the question in white bold capitals, lower half.
- Then small white lowercase subtitles, 1-4 words, bold key word, yellow italics for numbers and quotes. Almost no graphics. Cut only between a wide and a close framing (crop a 4K shot).
- The box is what stops the scroll: it names the question before he says a word. Test it against the same clip without the box and keep whichever does better for you.
- The car preset: phone on the dash, parked, one take.
- Best for: answering a viewer's question, opinions.

## 5. Walk & Show
- Handheld selfie, often moving to a new spot for each point.
- Captions: 1-3 words, white bold with a thin dark outline, centred mid-frame, small; yellow on money and time. A "POV:" line at the top on story videos.
- Very light graphics: real dashboard or screen cards held 1-2 seconds.
- Best for: lists on the move, stories, behind the scenes.

## 6. Clean Wide
- One tripod, one clean bright spot, one take, centred. Text on screen from frame 0, word-by-word white lowercase captions, small cards over your chest.
- The top outliers for this style are 10-31 seconds with no cuts at all.
- Best for: short lists and price-tag style breakdowns.

## Devices (use inside any preset)
- **Reel wall:** when you talk about views or content, the background becomes a slow-scrolling wall of reels with their view counts, you cut out in front.
- **Feed scroll:** when you talk about one creator, their feed scrolls behind you.
- **Reel orbit:** a few reels circle above your head, passing behind it.
- **Pyramid:** for any 3-level framework, one triangle splits into bands, each lights up and labels itself as you say it, and the camera pushes in.

Code for every preset and device, plus the captions, text hook, opener, breathe and transitions, is in `editor/` (see `editor/README.md`).
