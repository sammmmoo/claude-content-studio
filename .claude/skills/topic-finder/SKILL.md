# topic-finder

**Outcome:** a short list of video ideas, each one copied from a real outlier and moved into the creator's niche, so every idea starts with evidence.

## Where ideas come from (in this order)

1. **Outliers** in `references/OUTLIERS.md` (3x+ the creator's median). Run research-creators first if it's empty.
2. **The creator's own winners**: ask which of their videos did best, and make follow-ups.
3. **Real questions** their customers or viewers ask. Ask them for the last five.

Never an idea from a blank page.

## For each idea

- **The idea in one line**, as their viewer would say it ("I'm working every night and still not making money").
- **Copied from:** the outlier (link, multiple) and which beats you're taking.
- **Format:** list, ranking, story, how-to, myth vs truth, before/after, reaction, POV, question answered. Use the one the outlier used.
- **Why their viewer cares:** what they get out of it in one line.
- **Where to film it:** what fits their setup in `my/style.md`.

## Filters (drop an idea that fails one)

- Would a stranger with this problem stop scrolling for it, even if they'd never heard of the creator?
- Can the creator say it truthfully, from their own experience?
- Is it about one thing? Two ideas make two videos.
- Does it need something they don't have (someone else's lifestyle, a team, fame)? Then skip it.

Mix the list: mostly ideas for their core viewer, some broader ones for reach, and the odd one that's pure entertainment. Say which you'd film first and why in one line.
